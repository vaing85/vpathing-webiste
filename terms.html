Call Assistant SMS notification program

Program name: VPathing Call Assistant Notifications.

Program description: Our AI call-assistant screens incoming phone calls and takes voicemails on your behalf. After a call is screened or a voicemail is recorded, we send an SMS text message to the mobile number you registered, summarizing who called and why. Messages are sent only to the account owner’s own registered mobile number.

How to opt in: You opt in by entering and saving your own mobile number as the notification number inside the Call Assistant application during account setup. By doing so, you consent to receive recurring SMS messages related to activity on your assistant line.

Message frequency: Recurring; frequency varies based on your call activity. You typically receive one message each time your assistant line screens a call or records a voicemail.

Cost: Message and data rates may apply. VPathing does not charge a fee for the notifications, but your mobile carrier’s standard rates apply.

To opt out: Reply STOP at any time to cancel SMS messages. After you send STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages.

For help: Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at villa.aing@vpathingenterprisellc.site.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to carrier availability and coverage.
