Pricing & Billing

Clear, transparent pricing for our software services and products. All prices are in U.S. dollars (USD).

How our pricing works

VPathing Enterprise LLC provides custom software development services and operates a number of software applications. Because our work ranges from custom builds to hosted apps, we price in the ways described below. Every engagement is confirmed with a written quote, order summary, or checkout total before you are charged, so you always know the exact amount and currency in advance.

What we offer

  • Custom software services

    Web apps, automation, dashboards, integrations, and MVP builds. Priced per project or per hour based on scope, and quoted before any work begins. Typical projects start at $1,500 USD.

  • Subscriptions

    Selected hosted applications are offered on a recurring monthly or annual plan. The exact price and billing interval are shown at sign-up and on your receipt. Plans start at $9 USD / month.

  • One-time purchases

    Certain products and licenses are available for a single, one-time fee shown at checkout before you pay.

Exact pricing for a specific app or project is provided on that product’s page, in your checkout summary, or in a written quote. For a custom quote, contact us.

Payment methods and processing

Payments are processed securely by Stripe, our third-party payment processor. We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover) and other payment methods supported by Stripe at checkout. VPathing Enterprise LLC does not store your full card details on our own servers.

Currency: All charges are billed in U.S. dollars (USD) unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Statement descriptor: Charges from us appear on your card or bank statement as VPATHING ENTERPRISE (or a similar descriptor identifying VPathing Enterprise LLC).

Taxes and renewals

  • Taxes. Prices are exclusive of applicable taxes unless otherwise noted. Any required sales tax or VAT is calculated and shown at checkout.
  • Subscription renewals. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price until canceled. You can cancel at any time; see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
  • Price changes. If we change the price of a subscription, we will notify active subscribers in advance, and the new price applies from your next renewal.

Refunds and cancellations

You can cancel subscriptions at any time and may be eligible for a refund under the terms of our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Please review that policy for full details on eligibility and how to request a refund.
