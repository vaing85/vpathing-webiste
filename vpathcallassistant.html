Call Assistant

An AI that answers and screens your calls — so you never miss what matters, and never pick up what doesn't.

It picks up the calls you'd otherwise miss, finds out who's calling and why, texts you a summary, and connects the ones worth your time. A personal spam screener and a full business receptionist in one.

Get started See how it works

How it works

  • You keep your number

    Set up simple carrier forwarding so only your missed, busy, or unreachable calls go to the assistant. Your phone still rings first — no loops, nothing to install for callers.

  • The AI answers & screens

    It greets the caller, asks who's calling and why, and handles it — takes a message, filters spam, or connects a real caller straight to you.

  • You get the summary

    A text and push notification tell you who called and what they needed, with a full transcript and recording in the app. Nothing important slips through.

Built for two jobs

  • For you

    Stop answering spam and robocalls. The assistant screens unknown callers, filters junk, and hands you a plain-English summary of the ones that are real — so your phone stops interrupting you.

  • For your business

    A professional receptionist that never misses a call. It answers as your business, screens and takes messages, connects live callers to you, and can run multiple lines with a department menu — at a fraction of an answering service.
