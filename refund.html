Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how VPathing Enterprise LLC (“VPathing,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles refunds, cancellations, and returns for the products and services we sell. It applies to all purchases made through our websites and applications. All prices and payments are in U.S. dollars (USD) unless stated otherwise, and payments are processed securely by our third-party payment processor, Stripe.

Subscriptions and recurring plans

  • Cancel anytime. You may cancel a subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting us. Cancellation stops future renewals; your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.
  • Renewals. Subscriptions renew automatically at the then-current price until canceled. We do not provide refunds for partial billing periods or for periods already elapsed.
  • New subscribers. If you are charged for a new subscription and have not made substantial use of the paid service, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we will review your request for a full or prorated refund in good faith.
  • Billing errors. If you were charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge or a charge after cancellation), contact us and we will refund the incorrect amount in full.

One-time and digital purchases

  • 14-day window. For one-time purchases of digital products or licenses, you may request a refund within 14 days of purchase, provided the product has not been substantially downloaded, activated, or used.
  • Non-refundable items. Products that have been fully delivered, consumed, or customized to your specifications may not be eligible for a refund, except where required by law.

Custom development and professional services

  • Before work begins. If you cancel a project or engagement before we begin work, any deposit or prepayment is fully refundable.
  • After work begins. Custom development, automation, integration, and consulting services are billed for time and work performed. Amounts covering work already completed or in progress are non-refundable. Any prepaid amounts for work not yet performed will be refunded, less costs already incurred.
  • Milestone projects. For milestone- or phase-based engagements, completed and accepted milestones are non-refundable. You may cancel remaining, un-started milestones for a refund of amounts prepaid toward them.

How to request a refund or cancellation

To request a refund or cancel a service, email us at villa.aing@vpathingenterprisellc.site or use our contact page. Please include your name, the email address used for the purchase, the product or service, and the date and amount of the charge so we can locate your order quickly.

How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through Stripe. Once approved, refunds are typically initiated within 5–10 business days. The time it takes for the funds to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer. We do not charge a fee to process a refund.

Questions and disputes

If you have a concern about a charge, please contact us first — we are committed to resolving billing issues promptly and fairly. Your statutory rights under applicable consumer protection laws are not affected by this policy.

Contact us

VPathing Enterprise LLC — villa.aing@vpathingenterprisellc.site. See our contact page for full support details.
