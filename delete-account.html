Delete your V Path Call Assistant account

How to delete your account for V Path Call Assistant, the Call Assistant app by V PATHing Enterprise LLC, and the data that goes with it.

How to request deletion

Email villa.aing@vpathingenterprisellc.site from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. Sending from your sign-in address is how we know the request is yours.

We will reply to confirm, and your account will be deleted within 72 hours.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

What we delete

  • Your account: your email address and name.
  • Your assistant settings and the instructions you gave your assistant.
  • Your assistant phone number(s). They are released and cannot be recovered.
  • Your screened calls, voicemails, call recordings, transcripts and summaries.
  • The notification tokens for your devices.

If you have an active subscription, it is cancelled as part of the deletion.

What we keep

Payment and billing records, such as invoices and payment history, which we must keep for tax and accounting purposes, generally for up to 7 years. These are held by our payment processor, Stripe. We never store your full card number.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can also ask us to delete specific data, such as particular call recordings, by emailing the same address from your sign-in email and saying what you want removed.
